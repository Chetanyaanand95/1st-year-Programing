Delhi Skill and Entrepreneurship University (DSEU)

About the Institution

Delhi Skill and Entrepreneurship University (DSEU) is a public state university in Delhi that provides quality education with practical and industry-oriented learning. The university focuses on skill development, innovation, and entrepreneurship.

  • BBA
  • MCA
  • B.Tech
